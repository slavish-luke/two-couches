Motion lab

Sixteen ways the app could move more. Each one is wired to something real in Two Couches, with real posters. Press its buttons, hover the posters, and try the dial at the bottom.

The Motion dial is a proposal too: a per-device setting next to Data saver. Calm is roughly the app today, Lively would be the new default, Wild turns everything up (screen shakes, confetti, comic sound words in POW!). Reduced motion on the device always wins.
