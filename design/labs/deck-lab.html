  • Which posters: the poster the app shows today on top (TMDB's pick for the language), then the most-voted posters in the viewer's language, then textless ones (for Turkish, English ones after that, since Turkish posters are rare). Textless first swaps the last two. Turn on Card labels to see each card's language and the TMDB size it loads: the cards underneath load a small size (w185), and the full one (w500) only when a card comes to the top.
  • Open questions: should a chosen poster be saved for the two of you (try "Use this one for us": it would then show on your list, the grids and Pick), or is shuffling just for fun? And how many cards (Cards, 2 to 6)?
  • Tut Sözünü has one poster on TMDB, so it shows the plain poster with no deck and no Shuffle button. The Inside Out 2 and House of the Dragon decks change most between English and Turkish.
